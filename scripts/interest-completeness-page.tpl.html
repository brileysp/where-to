  • How "done" is decided. Scores: every applicable place has one. Month curves: 90% of places have a hand-authored curve. Columns filled: 90% of places have a quarter or more of the cells filled, or are marked source-checked. Source-checked: 90% of places marked. Text written: 90% of places have a Gemini overview and months. Fact-checked: a dated fact-check note with its sources on the interest page, or every risky sentence checked. Expert review: at least one review by a person (Claude's own notes do not count). Riley reviewed? and the two Rank check columns are your own marks, not computed: click a cell to cycle not yet, partly, done. They are saved for everyone who opens the page. Rank check: similar places means the scores were ranked within kinds of places (for example fine dining among cities) and any that felt high or low were adjusted; all places means the peak scores were ranked across every place for the interest. Both are marked done for the seven interests Gemini wrote before 31 October, as you told me; the rest are blank until the check is done.
