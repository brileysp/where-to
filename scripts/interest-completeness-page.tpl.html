One row per interest, one column per main step, read from the live database on . Most complete at the top. A tick means the step is done for the interest; an amber pill shows how much is done; a faint dot means not started. Hover or long-press a cell for the exact numbers.

  • How "done" is decided. Scores: every applicable place has one. Month curves: 90% of places have a hand-authored curve. Columns filled: 90% of places have a quarter or more of the cells filled, or are marked source-checked. Source-checked: 90% of places marked. Text written: 90% of places have a Gemini overview and months. Fact-checked: every risky sentence checked. Expert review: at least one review by a person (Claude's own notes do not count).
  • Pass finished is bookkeeping: a pass is closed by hand once its run is done. Seventeen old passes were closed on 28 October after checking that no staged text was newer than the live text, so nothing was waiting to be applied.
  • Answers mapped shows partial when some questions are deliberately left unmapped (no data to read), so a full tick is not always the goal.
  • Interests with no columns yet (Landscape Photography, National Parks) show their chips and questions only. Road-Tripping (not an interest, just a way to see a place) and three internal sliders (Family Fun, Low-Season Deals, Avoiding Crowds) are left out.
