## Month scores
Also return monthly_scores: twelve numbers, January to December, from 0.0 to 10.0 with one decimal. Each is how good this interest is in this place in that month, for a traveler, on one scale used for every place in the world.
- 10.0 is for the best of the best: places a specialist would name on a world top list. It is rare, usually one to three months at most for a place, and most places never reach it.
- 8.5 to 9.7 is elite; 6.5 to 8.4 a strong regional place; 4.0 to 6.4 real but modest; 2.0 to 3.9 low; 0.5 to 1.9 a token or specialist option; 0.0 nothing available that month.
- A month's score follows what a visitor can actually do or see that month. Months that are genuinely the same get the same score. A gradual change should show as a gradual change (a few tenths a month), not as jumps: the app writes one text for each run of neighboring months whose scores stay close, so the score curve is what decides how many texts a place gets.
- Use all ten last digits about equally: 6.0, 6.1, 6.2 ... 6.9 are all equally normal, a 6.5 is no more or less natural than a 6.3. Do not lean on whole numbers and halves, and do not avoid them either. Two months should not share a score unless they are truly identical. Never score two neighboring months the same without a reason.
- Score the experience, not the weather outside. For an interest that happens indoors or all year (museums, architecture, restaurants, shopping, spas and the like), heat, cold and crowds do not make a month worse; only a real change in access does (a closure, reduced hours, a season when a site is shut). Such an interest should be close to flat across the year unless something real changes. Only outdoor and weather-driven interests swing with the seasons.
- If nothing changes through the year, a flat set of twelve identical scores is correct, at any value (7.5 as fine as 7.6).
- Score a place's honest standing for this interest, not its best single venue. A place that is not known for it, or known only for plain versions of it, scores in the low or modest range even if a few good places exist.
- Where the experience is mostly outdoors (open-air markets and stalls, terraces, vineyard and brewery visits), weather and the produce season do matter even when everything stays open: in temperate climates, cold and rainy months mean fewer vendors, fewer visitors and thinner produce, so those months score lower.
- The balance across last digits is checked and corrected by the pipeline on the whole batch (together with the scores already live), so do not try to steer digits yourself and do not adjust a score away from what the text says to hit a digit.
