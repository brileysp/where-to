You are researching **Kayaking & Rafting** as a travel interest for one specific
destination in a travel-recommendation app. Your job is to produce a month-by-month
(January through December) assessment of how good this destination genuinely is for
kayaking and/or rafting, grounded in real, verifiable facts — not a generic or invented
seasonal narrative.

"Kayaking & Rafting" covers: whitewater rafting and kayaking on rivers (rated by the
International Scale of River Difficulty, Class I-VI), sea/coastal kayaking, and flatwater
paddling where it's a genuine, well-known draw — not merely "there is a lake here." A
destination can be excellent for one of these and irrelevant for the others; say which
specifically applies.

## Kayaking & Rafting-specific guidance

- Name the actual river, put-in, coastline, or specific stretch — "the river here" is not
  specific enough for a monthly entry to stand alone.
- River-based whitewater is usually flow-dependent (snowmelt timing, dam-release
  schedules, or monsoon/rainy-season timing) — state what actually drives the season for
  THIS river, not a generic "spring is best for rivers" assumption.
- If whitewater difficulty is a well-documented, named grade (e.g. "Class IV-V"), include
  it — it's exactly the kind of specific, verifiable fact this content needs.
- Sea kayaking's season is usually about calm-water windows and wildlife, not flow — treat
  it as its own distinct case, not a smaller version of a river destination.
- A destination with only mild, tourist-flatwater paddling and no genuine whitewater or
  notable coastal kayaking reputation should score modestly across the board, honestly —
  don't manufacture a narrower "peak" than actually exists.

## Output

Return the structured JSON this call's schema requires: `overview` (2-3 sentences, the
non-seasonal summary of what this destination offers for kayaking/rafting overall),
`monthly` (exactly 12 entries, January through December, each with a `score` 0-10 and a
`text` describing that month specifically), `sources` (real URLs you're grounding claims
in, if you have them), `confidence` (your own honest assessment: "low" if you're unsure
real kayaking/rafting infrastructure exists here at all), and `flags` (anything uncertain,
or any current-conditions concern like a closure — never woven into the content itself,
per the voice rules above).
