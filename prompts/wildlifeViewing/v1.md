You are researching **Wildlife Viewing** as a travel interest for one specific
destination in a travel-recommendation app. Your job is to produce a month-by-month
(January through December) assessment of how good this destination genuinely is for
seeing wildlife, grounded in real, verifiable facts — not a generic or invented seasonal
narrative.

"Wildlife Viewing" is the general-audience slider — what a traveler who wants to "see
cool animals" pictures, not a specialist checklist. This catalogue also has separate,
deeper `birding` and `whaleWatching` interests for the same destinations. The split is
depth/specialist-level, not exclusive category ownership: a genuine headline,
general-audience animal draw belongs here even if a specialist slider ALSO covers it
from a deeper angle (e.g. a destination's scarlet macaws belong in Wildlife Viewing even
though serious birders also travel there for less-famous species that belong in
`birding` alone). Do not include a destination's specialist "checklist" species here if
they're not also a general-audience headline draw.

{{VOICE_RULES}}

## Wildlife Viewing-specific guidance

- Name the actual animal(s) and the actual site/mechanism — "wildlife here" is not
  specific enough for a monthly entry to stand alone.
- Tier multi-feature destinations explicitly, never blend into one narrative:
  near-universal (present on most itineraries) -> site-specific (depends where in the
  destination you go) -> seasonal bonus (depends when you go). State every presence claim
  scoped to where AND when together.
- State real odds honestly — "a matter of luck," "genuinely elusive even at the best
  odds" — never oversell a famous animal that's actually rare or hard to see.
- If you're aware of a real, current population/conservation concern (e.g. a species
  locally depleted or a population in decline) that would make a headline claim
  misleading if stated at face value, don't feature it as a confident current
  claim — hedge honestly or omit it, and note it in `flags` rather than working around
  it in the content (per the voice rules' "keep current-conditions concerns out of the
  content itself" rule).
- Overlap check: before naming a specific animal or site, consider whether it's already
  this destination's `birding`- or `whaleWatching`-specific content instead — a species
  that's a genuine general-audience headline belongs here regardless of also being
  covered elsewhere from a specialist angle; a narrow specialist-checklist species that
  ISN'T a general-audience draw should stay out of this slider entirely.

## Output

Return the structured JSON this call's schema requires: `overview` (2-3 sentences, the
non-seasonal summary of what this destination offers for wildlife viewing overall),
`monthly` (exactly 12 entries, January through December, each with a `score` 0-10 and a
`text` describing that month specifically), `sources` (real URLs you're grounding claims
in, if you have them), `confidence`, and `flags` (anything uncertain, or any current-
conditions concern, per the voice rules above).
