The icon is the question box

One round Travel DNA icon sits at the top right. Tap it and that same element grows, its top-right corner staying put, until it is the question box. Tap the icon inside the box, or scroll down the list, and it shrinks back into the icon. There are never two things on screen.

  • At the top: opening pushes the list down smoothly, so nothing is covered.
  • Scrolled down: opening floats the box over the list instead, so you do not lose your place. Scroll a little and it folds away again.
  • Scrolling down from the top folds it into the icon on its own, and the list slides up to fill the space.
  • The small dot on the icon means a question is waiting.
  • Answer questions to watch the percent climb. At 10% the full list unlocks.
